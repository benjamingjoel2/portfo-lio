about

tourground is the modern infrastructure for travel agents, dmcs and tour operators. it gives them the network, the technology and the distribution to run a tour operation: a verified profile, one inbox for every inquiry, quotes and payments in a link, and reach across websites, ai chats and whatsapp.

for travellers it works the other way round: tourground matches you with a trusted local agent, and your agent plans the trip.

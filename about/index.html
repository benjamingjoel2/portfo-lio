pixels, places
& travel tech

hi, i’m benjamin. i’m a designer and developer in travel tech, working full time at tourground, where i design, build and ship the websites for its travel brands.

the part i like most sits right between design and code: drawing a layout, building it properly, then getting it live, fast and easy to read on a phone.

i work from everywhere. right now that’s kaduna, nigeria, and i’m packing for indonesia.

and travel. that gets its own section.

building for travel

every site starts with one destination and the specialists who plan it. my job is to make that place feel worth the trip before anyone reads a word.

so the photos lead, the type stays quiet, and the trip pages read like a conversation with the planner.

travellers browse on phones, often on slow connections, often far from home. the pages are static, light and quick, and they work for everyone.

i write the front end by hand and ship it myself, so nothing gets lost between the design and the live site.

so far that’s 7 brands: antarctica, australia, italy, iceland, the uae, japan and benin.

each one is in my work.
